Quick flash

The classic one-button installer (ESP Web Tools). It writes the firmware, then offers to connect the display to Wi-Fi over USB. Finish everything else, like location, weather and the scope layout, on the device or in its settings portal: the address is on the display under Settings → Portal.

⚠️

This browser can’t flash over USB. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.
⚠️

Web Serial needs a secure page (https). Open this installer from its published address.
Prefer to set everything up first? The full installer writes your Wi-Fi, location, API key and scope layout together with the firmware.
After flashing
  1. Accept the safety notice on the display.
  2. If you skipped Wi-Fi here, the display shows a QR code for its FlightScnr-XXXX hotspot. Join it with your phone and pick your network.
  3. Set your location in the portal. Everything else has sensible defaults.
