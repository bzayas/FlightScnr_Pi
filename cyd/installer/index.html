FlightScnr for CYD
Firmware — Quick flash GitHub ↗

    FlightScnr CYD is a port of FlightScnr Pi by Yash Mulgaonkar, licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Personal, non-commercial use only. Commercial use needs separate permission from the author.

    Not for navigation or any safety-critical use. Flight data: adsb.fi, airplanes.live, adsb.lol (community feeds), routes from adsbdb.com. Weather: Tomorrow.io or Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0). Earthquakes: USGS. Airports and runways: OurAirports (public domain). Tower audio: LiveATC.net, personal listening only.

    Flashing uses esptool-js (Apache-2.0), loaded from unpkg when you press Connect. Nothing you enter here leaves your browser except place searches (Open-Meteo), naming your current location (BigDataCloud) and the optional Tomorrow.io key test.
